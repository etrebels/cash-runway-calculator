# Support

This is a **reference implementation, offered as-is with best-effort support.**

- **Questions & bugs:** open a [GitHub issue](../../issues). We read them and respond when we can, but there is no guaranteed response time or SLA.
- **Security issues:** please do not open a public issue. Email security@langoptima.com with details.
- **Commercial support / the hosted tools:** the maintained, hosted versions live at [tools.langoptima.com](https://tools.langoptima.com). For help applying these models to your organization, see [langoptima.com](https://www.langoptima.com).

We maintain this repository actively, but community contributions may take time to review.
